Vector search finds items whose embeddings are close to the embedding of a query. Embedding models map text to high dimensional vectors so that sentences with similar meaning end up near each other. Closeness is usually measured with cosine similarity or the inner product of normalised vectors. Libraries such as FAISS provide fast nearest neighbour search, from exact flat indexes to approximate indexes like IVF and HNSW that trade a little accuracy for large speedups on millions of vectors. Dense retrieval is strong at matching meaning, for example finding a passage about cars when the question mentions automobiles. Its weakness is exact terms such as product codes, rare names or error numbers, which an embedding may blur. Embedding models distinguish between documents and queries, so a query is embedded with a search query input type while stored chunks use a search document input type.
